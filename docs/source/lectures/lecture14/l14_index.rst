====================================================
Week 14: PT5 Lab
====================================================

Overview
--------

Dedicated in-class lab time for the PT5 capstone team project. No new
material is presented; the instructor circulates to consult with teams,
unblock specific issues, and review architecture decisions in the home
stretch before the Week 15 demo.


.. admonition:: Suggested Use of Lab Time
   :class: learning-objectives

   - Run your PT5 system end-to-end on a sample ARIAC scenario; identify
     the slowest stage and the most fragile one.
   - Walk through the recovery patterns from Week 12 and confirm each ARIAC
     challenge type (faulty parts, sensor blackouts, high-priority orders)
     is handled.
   - Rehearse the Week 15 demo: who narrates, who drives, what to do when
     it fails on stage.
   - Consult the instructor for feedback on architecture and recovery.


Topics

- (No new material.)
- Team work blocks; instructor consultations.


Hands-On Exercise
-----------------

PT5 integration and debugging in your team.


ARIAC Tie-in
------------

PT5 IS the ARIAC tie-in for this week. Use the lab time to stress-test your
integrated system against the published ARIAC challenge set.
