====================================================
Week 15: PT5 Demo and Presentation
====================================================

Overview
--------

The PT5 capstone demo day. Each team runs their integrated AI-augmented
robotic system on an unseen ARIAC scenario, in front of the class, and
delivers a short presentation (5 to 7 minutes) covering architecture,
design decisions, failure modes encountered, and how they were handled.
PT5 is due today.


.. admonition:: Demo Day Logistics
   :class: learning-objectives

   - Each team is assigned a demo slot (TODO: schedule).
   - The exact ARIAC scenario is revealed at the start of the team's slot.
   - Teams have a fixed amount of time to bring up the system and run.
   - Grading covers performance (against the unseen scenario), the
     presentation, and the team's defense of their design choices in Q&A.


Presentation Outline (5 to 7 minutes)
-------------------------------------

- **Architecture** (1 minute): block diagram, layer mapping (deliberative,
  executive, reactive), where AI plugs in.
- **Design decisions** (2 minutes): the two or three key choices your team
  made and why (PDDL vs. LLM for which scenarios, VLM grounding strategy,
  recovery patterns chosen).
- **Live demo** (2 to 3 minutes): system runs on the unseen scenario.
- **Failure modes** (1 minute): what failed in development and how you
  fixed it.


Hands-On Exercise
-----------------

The demo itself is the hands-on. No additional exercise.
