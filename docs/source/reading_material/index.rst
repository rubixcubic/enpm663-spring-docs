====================================================
Reading Material
====================================================

Supplemental primers that support the lectures. Where a topic is a prerequisite
refresher (rather than new course content), it is provided as pre-recorded
material on ELMS-Canvas and students are expected to review it outside of class
by the recommended time.

.. list-table::
   :widths: 32 44 24
   :header-rows: 1
   :class: compact-table

   * - Topic
     - Covers
     - When to review
   * - ROS 2 Refresher
     - Lifecycle nodes, TF2, QoS profiles, and executors, at the depth the
       course relies on.
     - Before Week 1
   * - PDDL Primer
     - PDDL domains and problems, typed predicates, and running a planner from
       Python.
     - Before Week 6
   * - LLM Prompting and Structured Output
     - System vs. user prompts, JSON-schema-constrained output, validation and
       retry, and tool / function calling.
     - Before Week 9
   * - Model Context Protocol (MCP)
     - How MCP standardizes tool and context integration for LLM agents.
     - Before Week 9

.. note::

   These primers are being authored for the Spring 2027 offering. Links to the
   recorded material and any written notes will be posted on ELMS-Canvas.
